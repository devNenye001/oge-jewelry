JEWELRY CARE: KEEP YOUR
PIECES LOOKING THEIR BEST

Your jewelry carries your story. Here’s how to keep every piece looking beautiful for years to come.

Jewelry is made to be worn, loved, and remembered. But just like anything meaningful, your pieces need a little care to keep their beauty intact.

Whether it’s an everyday necklace, a treasured ring, or a piece from one of our signature collections, a few simple habits can help preserve its finish and keep it looking its best.

WEAR WITH INTENTION

Your jewelry is designed to become part of your everyday life, but certain environments can affect its appearance over time.

We recommend removing your jewelry before:

  • Showering or bathing
  • Swimming
  • Exercising
  • Sleeping
  • Applying perfume, lotions, or oils
  • Using household cleaning products

Perfumes, skincare products, sweat, chlorine, and harsh chemicals can affect the finish of your jewelry, particularly gold vermeil and gold-plated pieces.

A simple rule to remember:
Jewelry goes on last and comes off first.

KEEP IT AWAY FROM WATER AND CHEMICALS

Water itself isn’t always harmful, but repeated exposure to moisture can contribute to tarnishing or changes in appearance depending on the material.

Chlorinated and salt water can be particularly harsh on jewelry.

Try to keep your pieces away from:

  • Chlorine
  • Salt water
  • Household chemicals
  • Perfume
  • Hairspray
  • Lotions and oils

Allow skincare and fragrance products to fully dry before putting on your jewelry.

CLEAN GENTLY

Your jewelry doesn’t need aggressive cleaning to stay beautiful.

After wearing your pieces, gently wipe them with a soft, clean, dry cloth to remove oils and residue.

For deeper cleaning, follow the care instructions provided with your specific piece. Avoid abrasive cloths, harsh chemicals, or strong cleaning solutions unless they are specifically recommended for the material.

When in doubt, gentle is always better.

STORE YOUR PIECES PROPERLY

How you store your jewelry matters just as much as how you wear it.

Keep your pieces:

  • In a cool, dry place
  • Away from direct sunlight and humidity
  • Separately stored to prevent scratching and tangling
  • Inside their original packaging or a soft jewelry pouch where possible

Necklaces should ideally be stored in a way that prevents them from becoming tangled, while delicate pieces should have enough space to avoid rubbing against one another.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR MATERIALS

Different materials naturally behave differently over time.

GOLD VERMEIL

Gold vermeil consists of a layer of gold over sterling silver. With regular wear, the gold surface can gradually change depending on exposure, friction, and how the piece is cared for.
Proper storage and gentle wear can help preserve its finish.

GOLD-PLATED JEWELRY

Gold-plated jewelry has a layer of gold over another base metal. The plating can naturally wear down over time, particularly in areas exposed to frequent friction or moisture.
Careful handling can help extend the life of the finish.

STERLING SILVER

Sterling silver can naturally tarnish when exposed to air, moisture, and certain chemicals. This is a normal characteristic of the material and does not necessarily mean that the piece is damaged.
Gentle cleaning and proper storage can help maintain its appearance.

NATURAL STONES

Natural stones have their own unique characteristics, which means no two pieces may look exactly alike.
Avoid exposing stone-set jewelry to harsh chemicals, extreme temperatures, or unnecessary impact. When cleaning, use gentle methods appropriate for the specific stone.

A LITTLE CARE GOES A LONG WAY

The beauty of jewelry is that it becomes part of your story. The pieces you wear often become connected to moments, milestones, and memories.
Taking a few extra moments to care for them helps ensure they remain part of those stories for years to come.

Wear your pieces. Love them. Care for them. And let them become part of your becoming.
